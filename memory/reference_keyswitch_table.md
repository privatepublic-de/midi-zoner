Note numbering convention: **0 = C-2** (Middle C = C3, Yamaha-style).

| Hex | Dec | Note | Keyswitch Action |
|-----|-----|------|-----------------|
| 0x00 | 0 | C-2 | Toggle mute — zone 1 |
| 0x01 | 1 | C#-2 | Toggle mute — zone 2 |
| 0x02 | 2 | D-2 | Toggle mute — zone 3 |
| 0x03 | 3 | D#-2 | Toggle mute — zone 4 |
| 0x04 | 4 | E-2 | Toggle mute — zone 5 |
| 0x05 | 5 | F-2 | Toggle mute — zone 6 |
| 0x06 | 6 | F#-2 | Toggle mute — zone 7 |
| 0x07 | 7 | G-2 | Toggle mute — zone 8 |
| 0x08 | 8 | G#-2 | Toggle sequencer — zone 1 |
| 0x09 | 9 | A-2 | Toggle sequencer — zone 2 |
| 0x0A | 10 | A#-2 | Toggle sequencer — zone 3 |
| 0x0B | 11 | B-2 | Toggle sequencer — zone 4 |
| 0x0C | 12 | C-1 | Toggle sequencer — zone 5 |
| 0x0D | 13 | C#-1 | Toggle sequencer — zone 6 |
| 0x0E | 14 | D-1 | Toggle sequencer — zone 7 |
| 0x0F | 15 | D#-1 | Toggle sequencer — zone 8 |
| 0x10 | 16 | E-1 | Select arrangement A |
| 0x11 | 17 | F-1 | Select arrangement B |
| 0x12 | 18 | F#-1 | Select arrangement C |
| 0x13 | 19 | G-1 | Select arrangement D |
